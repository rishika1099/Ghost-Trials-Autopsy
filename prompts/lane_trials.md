Your lane: TRIALS (ClinicalTrials.gov and Drugs@FDA).
Report, per trial: status, the stated why_stopped text quoted exactly and its category, start, primary completion and completion dates, enrollment, whether results are posted on ClinicalTrials.gov and when, results_due_date and days_late (an approximation: primary completion + 365 days), and whether the drug was ever approved by the FDA (get_fda_approvals) if the brief asks.
For trials the brief names by NCT ID, call get_trial_live to confirm the snapshot, and say if the live record differs. For a sponsor, condition or other list question, answer from query_trials_sql: one query for the rows and, if counts are asked, one for the counts. Do not call get_trial_live on each row of a list; the SQL rows are already cited records. Write your findings as soon as you have the rows. For trials outside the snapshot, get_trial_live is the only source: say so in notes.
Use get_snapshot_stats when a base rate would put the trial in context (for example the share of stopped trials with a blank reason).
If a record has start_date_precision (or primary_completion_date_precision, completion_date_precision) set to "month", the registry gives only the month: write that date as a month and year ("January 2015"), never as a full day.
