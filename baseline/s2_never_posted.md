### 1. Why Was the Trial Terminated?
The official reason provided by the sponsor on ClinicalTrials.gov for the termination of NCT04903327 is:
> **"Sponsor decision to terminate the study due to business reasons."**

While "business reasons" is a broad term, the termination is tied to two major factors:

*   **Financial Distress and Bankruptcy:** In February 2023, Sorrento Therapeutics filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection following a devastating legal dispute and a multi-million dollar arbitration loss against NantCell (associated with Patrick Soon-Shiong). To conserve cash and restructure, Sorrento was forced to halt, scale back, or terminate several of its clinical pipelines, including its COVID-19 programs.
*   **Declining Recruitment and Changing Pandemic Dynamics:** Like many other pharmaceutical companies developing COVID-19 therapeutics later in the pandemic, Sorrento faced challenges recruiting eligible patients. With the rollout of vaccines, milder dominant variants (like Omicron), and established standards of care, the pool of patients experiencing severe COVID-19-induced Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS) who met the trial’s strict enrollment criteria dropped significantly.

### 2. Were the Results Ever Posted or Published?

*   **In Peer-Reviewed Journals:** **No.** There are no published, peer-reviewed scientific papers detailing the efficacy or safety outcomes of the Phase 2 NCT04903327 trial.
