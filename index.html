Ghost Trials Autopsy:
stopped early, then what?

Drug companies end trials early all the time. Afterwards, the public registry, the investor filings and the medical journals can tell three different stories, or say nothing at all. Name a sponsor, a drug or a trial, and a team of research agents pulls all three records and lays them side by side.

For health reporters, patient advocates and research-integrity work.

What we check, every case

  1. Why did it stop?The status and reason the sponsor gave the registry
  2. Were the results ever shared?Posted on ClinicalTrials.gov, published in a journal, and how late
  3. What did investors hear?8-K and other SEC filings near the stop date
  4. Do the stories match?Where the registry, the filings and the papers disagree
