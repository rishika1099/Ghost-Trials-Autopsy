Ghost Trials Autopsy

Deep research agent

When a drug company stops a clinical trial early, what did it tell the public registry, its investors, and the scientific record? This agent pulls all three and checks whether the stories match.

What it answers

  • Why did the trial stop?
  • Were the results ever reported?
  • What did the sponsor tell investors?
  • Do those stories match?

Who it's for

Health reporters, patient advocates, and research-integrity people looking into a drug, a sponsor, a condition, or a trial ID (NCT number).

How to use it

Name a sponsor, drug, condition or NCT ID and what you want to know. Vague questions get a follow-up question first. Click any 1 to see its source. A full answer takes 1 to 4 minutes.
