# Sorrento Therapeutics terminated its phase 2 COVI-MSC trial NCT04903327. Why did it stop, and were the results ever posted or published?

## Answer
Sorrento Therapeutics terminated its Phase 2 COVI-MSC clinical trial NCT04903327 on 2022-11-23 due to slow enrollment and an inability to reach enrollment targets. [1] The trial had enrolled 43 participants before termination, but no results have been posted to ClinicalTrials.gov. [1]

## Timeline
Trial NCT04903327 started on 2021-11-16, was terminated on 2022-11-23, had results due on 2023-08-29, and has never posted results or published primary findings as of November 2026.

## Evidence
- Trial NCT04903327 was a Phase 2 trial sponsored by Sorrento Therapeutics, Inc. that reached a terminated status. [1]
- The trial officially started on 2021-11-16 and was terminated on 2022-11-23 with a primary completion date of 2022-08-29. [1]
- A total of 43 participants were enrolled in the trial prior to its termination. [1]
- The registered reason for termination was because of considerably slow enrollment and an inability to reach enrollment targets. [1]
- Trial results were due to be posted on ClinicalTrials.gov by 2023-08-29, but none have been posted. [1]
- Across the registry database, 46.3 percent of stopped trials have no results posted, and 630 stopped trials were stopped because of enrollment issues. [2]
- Five publications indexed in PubMed and Europe PMC mention the trial or the drug COVI-MSC. [3][4][5][6][7]
