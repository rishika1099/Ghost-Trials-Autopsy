# Did Sorrento Therapeutics break the law by not posting results for its terminated COVID-19 trials?

## Answer
Whether Sorrento Therapeutics broke the law cannot be determined from the available data. [1][2] The registry shows that Sorrento Therapeutics terminated two COVID-19 trials with participants enrolled, NCT04900428 and NCT04903327, but has not posted results for either study despite both passing their estimated results due dates. [1][2] Specifically, NCT04900428 had an enrollment of 179 and its results were due on 2023-03-22, while NCT04903327 had an enrollment of 43 and its results were due on 2023-08-29. [1][2]

## Timeline
Trial NCT04900428 started on 2021-07-16, was terminated on 2022-05-06 with a primary completion date of 2022-03-22, and had its results due on 2023-03-22, with no results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov and only a Cochrane review mentioning it on 2021-09-02. Trial NCT04903327 started on 2021-11-16, was terminated on 2022-11-23 with a primary completion date of 2022-08-29, and had its results due on 2023-08-29, with no results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov and only broad literature mentions from 2021 to 2023.

## Evidence
- Trial NCT04900428 was registered on ClinicalTrials.gov, started on 2021-07-16, and was terminated after enrolling 179 participants. [1]
- Trial NCT04900428 reached its primary completion date on 2022-03-22, making its estimated results due date 2023-03-22, but no results have been posted. [1]
- The registry lists the why_stopped reason for trial NCT04900428 as being terminated at interim analysis due to drops not working on new variants. [1]
- Trial NCT04903327 was registered on ClinicalTrials.gov, started on 2021-11-16, and was terminated after enrolling 43 participants. [2]
- Trial NCT04903327 reached its primary completion date on 2022-08-29, making its estimated results due date 2023-08-29, but no results have been posted. [2]
- The registry lists the why_stopped reason for trial NCT04903327 as unable to reach enrollment targets due to considerably slow enrollment. [2]
- PubMed lists one Cochrane systematic review published on 2021-09-02 that mentions the trial identifier for NCT04900428 but does not report trial-specific results. [3]
- PubMed lists five literature mentions for NCT04903327 published between 2021 and 2023, but none of these publications report specific results or conclusions for the trial. [4][5][6][7][8]
- A query of the SEC filings database in the snapshot returned zero rows. [9][10]

## What the data can't tell us
- We cannot tell if Sorrento Therapeutics broke the law because the estimated results due date is only an approximation of the FDAAA deadline, which applies only to applicable clinical trials whose status is not fully public.
- The database cannot tell us the real reasons why Sorrento Therapeutics terminated these trials beyond the brief explanations provided in the registry.
- We cannot tell whether the lack of posted results or published manuscripts was intended to mislead investors, patient advocates, or research-integrity investigators.
- Because the database snapshot contains zero filings in the SEC table and live SEC searching was unavailable, we cannot tell what Sorrento Therapeutics disclosed to its investors regarding these trials.
- There are no differences to compare because no primary trial results publications or investor filings with trial details were found in the database.
- The investigation stopped early due to the three-round research cap, leaving some external or live resources unsearched.
