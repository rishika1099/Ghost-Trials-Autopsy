# Did Sorrento Therapeutics break the law by not posting results for its terminated COVID-19 trials?

## Answer
Whether Sorrento Therapeutics has reporting discrepancies by not posting results for its terminated COVID-19 trials is a determination that cannot be made from these records, but the clinical trial registry shows that the results are long overdue. [1][2] For the terminated trial NCT04900428, results were due on 2023-03-22 and are 1324 days late. [1] For the terminated trial NCT04903327, results were due on 2023-08-29 and are 1164 days late. [2]

## Timeline
NCT04900428 (COVI-DROPS) started on 2021-07-16 and was terminated with a completion date of 2022-05-06, with results due on 2023-03-22; no results have been posted, and no 8-K filings or publications reported trial outcomes. NCT04903327 (COVI-MSC) started on 2021-11-16 and was terminated with a completion date of 2022-11-23, with results due on 2023-08-29; no results have been posted, though 8-K filings in February and March 2023 listed the drug in Sorrento's pipeline and several review articles from 2021 to 2023 mentioned the trial without reporting clinical outcomes.

## Evidence
- Sorrento Therapeutics registered the Phase 2 trial NCT04900428 (COVI-DROPS), which enrolled 179 participants and was terminated on 2022-05-06 because the drops did not work on new variants. [1]
- Sorrento registered the Phase 2 trial NCT04903327 (COVI-MSC), which enrolled 43 participants and was terminated on 2022-11-23 due to slow enrollment. [2]
- Neither NCT04900428 nor NCT04903327 has results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov. [1][2]
- No SEC Form 8-K filings for Sorrento Therapeutics from November 2021 to May 2023 mentioned NCT04900428 or COVI-DROPS. [3]
- SEC Form 8-K filings from February and March 2023 mentioned COVI-MSC as a trademark and a pipeline candidate but did not discuss the trial termination or outcomes. [4][5][6]
- No peer-reviewed publications reporting clinical trial outcomes for either trial were found in PubMed or Europe PMC. [7][8][9][10][11][12]
- A systematic review in PubMed lists NCT04900428 as a covered trial but does not report its primary clinical results. [7]
- Five publications mentioning NCT04903327 in Europe PMC are general reviews or protocol mentions rather than clinical outcome reports. [8][9][10][11][12]

## What the data can't tell us
- The registry does not provide the real reason for stopping beyond the stated text.
- A lack of clinical outcomes in SEC filings or publications is not proof of an intent to mislead.
- The results due date is an approximation of the FDAAA deadline, which only applies to applicable clinical trials, a status that is not fully public, so the delayed reporting is not evidence that Sorrento Therapeutics broke the law.
- Results published in conference abstracts, non-indexed journals, or company websites may have been missed.
- Trials outside the snapshot scope were only looked up live, or not at all.
- We cannot compare the registry termination reasons with the SEC filings and publications because the latter sources did not report any termination details or outcomes for these trials.
