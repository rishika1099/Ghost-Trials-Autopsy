# Which stopped trials in the snapshot have an 8-K that gives a different reason than the registry? Start with the ones checked by hand.

## Answer
Five hand-checked stopped trials in the snapshot are labeled as having a discrepancy. [1][2] The stopped trials include NCT03980730, NCT05528861, NCT02227784, NCT02452528, and NCT04802837. [3][4][5][6][7] Across the broader snapshot, 43 trials are labeled as having a discrepancy. [1] Other stopped trials include NCT01891643, NCT03024177, NCT03645395, NCT03949673, NCT04419389, NCT01925092, and NCT05181995. [8][9][10][11][12][13][14]

## Timeline
For NCT03980730, the trial started on 2019-06-27, completed on 2021-01-29, had results due on 2021-12-01, and results were posted on 2022-01-21; the 8-K was filed on 2021-02-24, and no primary publication was found. For NCT05528861, the trial started on 2022-10-26, completed on 2024-04-18, had results due on 2024-12-26, and results were posted on 2024-09-27; the 8-K was filed on 2024-01-16, and no primary publication was found. For NCT02227784, the trial started in 2014-10, completed on 2015-12-01, had results due on 2016-11-30, and results were posted on 2018-03-22; the 8-K was filed on 2015-10-22, and a primary publication was published on 2017-06-01. For NCT02452528, the trial started in 2015-08, completed in 2016-12, had results due on 2017-12-01, and results were posted on 2017-11-01; the 8-K was filed on 2016-11-10, and no primary publication was found. For NCT04802837, the trial started on 2021-05-19, completed on 2022-09-28, had results due on 2022-09-17, and results were posted on 2023-08-21; the 8-K was filed on 2022-10-20, and no primary publication was found.

## Evidence
- For NCT03980730, the registry says the reason was for business reasons, while the 8-K says the study did not meet its primary objective. [3][15]
- For NCT05528861, the registry says development was no longer pursued, but the 8-K says the trials did not meet their primary endpoints. [4][16]
- For NCT02227784, the registry says termination was due to program termination, but the 8-K says the trials were terminated due to insufficient efficacy. [5][17]
- For NCT02452528, the registry says Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals decided to discontinue the trial, but the 8-K says the study was on hold due to questions from a nonclinical toxicology study. [6][18]
- For NCT04802837, the registry says the study was terminated in alignment with a corporate decision to pursue development with a partner, while the 8-K says the drug did not achieve the pre-specified superiority endpoint. [7][19]
- For NCT01891643, the registry says the reason was insufficient enrollment, but the 8-K says the addition of elotuzumab did not show a statistically significant improvement in progression-free survival. [8][20]
- For NCT03024177, the registry says the study was withdrawn due to company decision. [9][21]
- For NCT03645395, the registry says it was stopped due to sponsor decision, but the 8-K says the clinical studies were on partial clinical hold following a treatment-related fatality. [10][22]
- For NCT03949673, the registry says it was stopped due to lack of enrollment. [11][23]
- For NCT04419389, the registry says it was stopped due to sponsor decision. [12][24]
- For NCT01925092, the registry says it was stopped due to lack of enrollment. [13][25]
- For NCT05181995, the registry says it was stopped due to company decision, but the 8-K says NYX-783 did not demonstrate sufficient improvement on the study's primary endpoint. [14][26]

## Where the stories differ
- Specifically, the registry reasons for trials NCT03980730, NCT05528861, NCT02227784, and NCT04802837 mention general business decisions or program terminations, but their respective 8-Ks state that the drugs showed insufficient efficacy or failed to meet their primary objectives. [3][15][4][16][5][17][7][19]
- For NCT02452528, the registry lists a standard company decision to discontinue the trial, whereas the 8-K discloses that the study was placed on hold due to questions from a nonclinical toxicology study. [6][18]
- Stopped trials such as NCT01891643, NCT03645395, and NCT05181995 list enrollment shortages or company decisions in the registry despite investor disclosures revealing clinical holds, safety issues, or statistical failures. [8][20][10][22][11][23][12][24][13][25][14][26]

## What the data can't tell us
- We cannot tell the real reason a trial was stopped beyond the text reported in the registry and investor disclosures.
- A discrepancy between these sources is not definitive proof of intent to mislead.
- The results due date only approximates the FDAAA deadline, which applies specifically to applicable clinical trials, meaning these findings do not provide evidence that any sponsor broke the law.
- Some results published in conference abstracts, non-indexed journals, or company websites may have been missed.
- Private sponsors do not file reports with the SEC, so our analysis of investor documents is limited to public companies.
- Trials outside the scope of the snapshot were either looked up live or not evaluated at all.
